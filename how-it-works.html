Search, pick, share, and confirm. TeamLodgr handles everything from there.

Pick Your Hotel
Browse results featuring photos, star ratings, amenities, and price ranges. Found the right fit? Click "Share with Team" to lock it in as your team's official hotel pick.

Enter Team Emails
Enter your own name and email, then paste in your team members' email addresses. TeamLodgr will send each person a personalized invite with all the details they need.
